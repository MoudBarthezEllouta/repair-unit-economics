# Data provenance

All datasets in this repository are invented for this portfolio. They are generated
from explicit constants and seeded random numbers, or handwritten as test fixtures.
No employer extracts, real device identifiers, company SQL, supplier names, prices,
diagnostic dictionaries, or measured company distributions were used as inputs.
This is synthetic data, not anonymized production data.

The problem framing draws on general experience in repair operations. Code and
schemas were newly written for this demonstration with AI assistance. Example
results are simulation outputs and do not measure employer performance or realized
savings. The project is an independent portfolio implementation, not an employer
product or a claim of production deployment.
